Unleash the Power of Your Air-Gapped Data

Wrought Iron provides a comprehensive suite of modules designed to handle every aspect of data management, from secure infrastructure to advanced machine learning, all without external network access.

Infrastructure (wi connect)

The foundation for secure data management, handling connection lifecycle, storage optimization, and physical security. It operates directly on the file system, ideal for air-gapped environments.

Schema Management (wi schema)

Introspect, analyze, and safely evolve your database structures. Gain deep insights into column properties, relationships, and semi-structured data patterns.

Data Exploration (wi query)

Quickly inspect, filter, search, and understand data with high-speed retrieval and powerful boolean logic operations. Get immediate insights without complex SQL.

Analytics (wi aggregate)

Perform robust statistical analysis and aggregations, including groupby, pivot tables, and descriptive statistics, directly within your air-gapped environment.

Visualization (wi plot)

Generate ASCII/Unicode charts directly in the terminal or save them to files. Quickly visualize data distributions, trends, and relationships even without a GUI.

Data Wrangling (wi clean)

Clean, standardize, and transform messy data into a usable format. Handle missing values, duplicates, text harmonization, and formal validation.

Geospatial Analysis (wi geo)

Work with location-based data including offline geocoding, distance calculations, clustering, and export to standard geospatial formats like GeoJSON.

Machine Learning (wi ml)

Bring powerful predictive modeling, classification, regression, clustering, and anomaly detection capabilities directly to your air-gapped data.

Audit & Security (wi audit)

Ensure data integrity, track changes, protect sensitive information, and establish a verifiable chain of custody with cryptographic hashing and PII scanning.

Operations (wi ops)

Automate maintenance, orchestrate workflows, and monitor operational status with tools for scheduling tasks, pipeline execution, and data drift detection.

Collaboration (wi collab)

Facilitate teamwork and project management through knowledge sharing, configuration management, and project portability for air-gapped environments.

Reporting (wi report)

Generate comprehensive, human-readable reports and visualizations directly from your data, including EDA, comparison reports, and interactive maps, for actionable intelligence.
